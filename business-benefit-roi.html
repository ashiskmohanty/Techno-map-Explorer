02 | Benefit case

Value reaches beyond search time

The strongest business case combines direct productivity with avoided delivery risk and retained institutional knowledge.

01

Faster technical discovery

Natural-language search, filters, and process drill-down reduce time spent navigating transactions, spreadsheets, package lists, and disconnected documentation. Primary measure: successful queries and minutes to identify the correct object.

02

Safer impact analysis

Dependency maps, where-used traces, and ABAP/BW hierarchy reveal relationships before a change is designed. Primary measure: trace usage, escaped dependency defects, and rework hours.

03

Reduced key-person dependency

Corrections and team teachings turn individual knowledge into a shared, searchable asset. Primary measure: retained teachings, repeat-answer accuracy, and onboarding time.

04

Faster ICM resolution

Connected process context, object details, and dependency traces help support teams identify affected components and likely root causes sooner. Primary measure: mean time to resolution, investigation hours, and incident reopen rate.

05

Quicker onboarding of new members

A searchable view of processes, technical objects, and team teachings gives new members a guided path through the SAP landscape. Primary measure: time to independent delivery, training hours, and mentor effort.

06

Overall reduction in maintenance cost

Faster discovery, more reliable impact analysis, and reusable knowledge reduce recurring investigation, rework, and specialist support effort. Primary measure: maintenance hours, change rework, and cost per resolved request.

07

Better change governance

Usage, feedback, audit history, and repository refresh controls create an evidence trail for adoption and data quality. Primary measure: active users, feedback closure, refresh frequency, and audit completion.

03 | ROI method

Transparent, action-based estimation

Each qualifying action is assigned a net manual-effort saving. The total is converted to hours, discounted for uncertainty, and valued at a loaded labor rate.

Step 1

05 | Financial integrity

Benefit is not yet full ROI

The application currently labels the $2,488 estimate as ROI. Financially, it is a gross productivity benefit. A defensible ROI percentage requires the cost side of the business case.

Required formula
ROI % = (Total quantified benefits - Total costs) / Total costs x 100

Capture build effort, infrastructure, SAP connectivity, support, data stewardship, training, and change-management costs. Keep avoided-risk value separate unless it is supported by incident or rework evidence.

06 | Validation plan

Move from modeled value to proven value

A short measurement cycle can replace assumed minutes with observed baselines and establish a finance-ready benefit case.

Weeks 1-2

Baseline

Time a representative sample of manual searches, where-used investigations, exports, refreshes, and rebuilds. Record role and task complexity.

Weeks 3-6

Validate

Compare tool-assisted completion time, successful outcomes, rework, and user confidence. Segment repeat actions to avoid double counting.

Quarterly

Govern

Review rates and event weights, archive stale telemetry, track operating costs, and publish realized benefit with owner sign-off.

07 | Assumptions

Interpretation boundaries

  • Telemetry represents the latest available usage records, capped by the application at 50,000 events.
  • Each qualifying JSONL event counts once; an optional event quantity field is not used in ROI.
  • The 70% credibility factor discounts all modeled time equally and is not task-specific.
  • The $40 hourly rate is a configurable loaded rate, not a verified payroll or vendor rate.
  • Current usage is an early sample of two users over three active days and should not be extrapolated directly.
  • Repository refresh and rebuild savings are large contributors and should be validated first.
  • Teachings are valued from the current correction inventory, not historical teaching events.
  • Benefits from avoided defects, audit readiness, and faster onboarding are described but not monetized.
